Chicken Pasta Recipe

A delicious and easy chicken pasta recipy you can make at home.

Recipy information .

Prep Time
>
15 minutes
Cook Time
20 minutes
Servings
2 people
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • 200 gram pasta
  • 150 gram chicken breast
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 100ml cooking cream
  • 50g cheese
  • 1 table spoon of olive oil

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta according to the package instructions.
  2. Cut the chicken into small pieces and cook it in olive oil.
  3. Add garlic and cook for another minute.
  4. Add the cooking cream and mix well.
  5. Add the cooked pasta and cheese, then mix everything together.
